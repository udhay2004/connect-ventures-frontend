Who we are

The Connect Ventures story

Connect Ventures was founded by Dr. Anil Gupta on a simple conviction: global expansion should not be the exclusive privilege of large corporations. Indian businesses with world-class products deserve world-class international support, at pricing they can afford.

Headquartered in Union City, California, we help founders set up and run businesses abroad: company formation, cross-border structuring, international tax, banking, compliance, and the market connections that turn a registered entity into a working business.

Together with our globalization partner Comply Globally, an ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certified execution partner, and a network of 1,000+ accounting, legal and secretarial firms worldwide, we take clients from first conversation to first customer abroad.

Our mission

To make going global simple, compliant and affordable for every ambitious Indian business.

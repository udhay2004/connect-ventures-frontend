Getting structure, tax and compliance right in two countries at once

Dr. Anil GuptaFounder and Chairman, Connect Ventures · 2 min read

Consulting is the C that turns a market-entry plan into a legal and financial structure: the entity, its ownership, its tax position, its bank account, and the filings it will owe every year in the country you enter and in India.

The second country is the one most advisers forget. For an Indian parent, a US subsidiary starts as a Form ODI filing in India before it becomes an EIN in America. After that come the Annual Performance Report, due by 31 December, and the FLA return, due by 15 July, every year. Late reporting attracts a Late Submission Fee under RBI's 2022 overseas investment directions, and after three years the matter moves to compounding (RBI, 2022). On the American side, a foreign-owned company that misses Form 5472 faces a $25,000 penalty per form. A foreign accountant rarely watches the Indian deadlines, and an Indian CA rarely watches the American ones.

Our position: design one structure, once, for both sides. Consulting under the 5Cs covers entity selection and holding structure, the DTAA position, a registered agent or nominee director where local law requires one, remote corporate banking with EIN or UEN applications, and secretarial filings from Delaware franchise tax to UK Companies House. It extends to trademarks, contracts, Employer of Record hiring and trade finance.

Good advice changes results in Indian firms. In a randomised study of Indian textile companies, intensive management consulting raised productivity by 17 per cent in the first year (Bloom et al., 2013). The researchers traced the firms' earlier inaction to one main cause: they lacked information.

Clients usually come to us for the formation. The value shows up in year three, when every filing on both sides is still on time.
